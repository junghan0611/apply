* Summary

I build the joints between humans, AI agents, and real systems. At one end: a Zigbee hub written in
Zig and shipped to mass production, with the Go server and Flutter app I built on top of it. At the
other: an npm-published dispatch substrate that connects agent harnesses without taking ownership of
their transcripts, authentication, or runtime. The middle is protocols, services, semantic memory,
deployment, and a reproducible NixOS/Emacs environment where humans and agents share tools, an
inspectable timeline, and explicit boundaries.

I build before claiming expertise: make a small vertical work, find where it breaks, preserve the
evidence, and turn the recovery into a reproducible tool, gate, or operating policy. My current
focus is the system around models — harnesses, memory, permissions, evidence, and hand-offs — not
foundation-model training. The next boundary is carrying what I learned from personal memory and
long-running agent collaboration into enterprise data, governance, workflow, and domain ownership.
