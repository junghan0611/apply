* Summary

I work across one vertical rather than collecting disconnected stacks. At the physical end: a
Zigbee hub in Zig, shipped to mass production, with the Go server and Flutter app I built when the
original contract stopped at firmware. At the agent end: a published dispatch substrate whose
boundary was extended by a contributor I had never met. Between them are protocols, services,
deployment, semantic retrieval, reproducible NixOS environments, and explicit operational gates.

The common method is build → break → preserve evidence → encode the recovery as a tool or policy.
That has produced deterministic firmware state transitions, interchangeable cloud/local backends,
staged agent-platform upgrades, source-to-binary provenance, and work items another person can take
over. I use Go, Clojure, Zig, C, TypeScript, Nix, and Elisp where each layer requires them; the claim
is backed by public repositories and runnable entry points rather than a technology list alone.

- @@latex:\cvlabel{Systems and Backend}@@ — Go and Clojure services, protocol design, PostgreSQL and
