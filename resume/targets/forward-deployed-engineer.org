* Summary

The request I keep receiving from other teams is not "build me a dashboard." It is "make this run
without depending on me." I begin with the domain owner: the units, periods, inclusion rules, source
systems, and failure conditions their work actually obeys. Then I build a read-only evidence path or
a bounded agent surface in their language, with every figure traceable to its source record and a
stop condition when the contract breaks.

Two such workbenches are in daily company use: one over customer-support data and one aligning a
platform catalog, device logs, and runtime state on a single time axis. I can cross the boundary
between field and engineering because I have built the layers underneath it — firmware, protocols,
backend services, deployment, and agent harnesses — and because I leave a hand-off another person
can inspect rather than a conversation only its author understands.

- @@latex:\cvlabel{Domain-Owner Deployment}@@ — operational language discovery, live-system

  integration is designed by someone who has had to make each layer work.
